A hex-tactics courier runSkyworks Concourse

The beacon that guided every freighter home across the cloud sea went dark a month ago. You are a courier carrying the micro-core that can relight it, landing on a cargo concourse whose drones have stopped answering to anyone.

Four districts stand between you and the summit. Each is a hex board fought turn by turn: move along the deck, strike from cover, mind the steam vents, and bring the Harbour Ring, Foundry Deck and Observatory Gardens into line before the Beacon Spire.

Everything is drawn and synthesised in the browser. Click once to enable sound.
