An isometric courier runSkyworks Concourse

Three decks of brass and glass hang over an endless cloud sea. Once the Skyworks beacon guided every freighter home; tonight it is dark, and the last courier shuttle will not wait.

You carry a micro‑core for the Harbour Chief. Cross the Foundry Deck for a focusing lens, align the mirrors of the Observatory Gardens, and bring the beacon back online before the shuttle departs.

Drones patrol the catwalks and scuttlers haunt the furnaces. Spend the capacitor shards you find at the Quartermaster to toughen your suit.
